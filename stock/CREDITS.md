# Tree section stock images

All images are from Wikimedia Commons and are public domain or CC0. make_moss.py cut 10 moss pieces from each of the 3 moss photos and assembled the TOUCH GRASS letters from them (stock/title-*.png). make_stock.py composited the ground strip (stock/ground.png), and make_moss.py pixelated it (stock/ground-px.png). The tree sprites come from rainbowroar14's own Sprout repo, and the signpost is the user's reference image.
