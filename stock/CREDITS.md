All images are from Wikimedia Commons and are public domain or CC0. They were cut out, recoloured and composited by make_stock.py into stock/title-*.png, stock/border-top.png and stock/ground.png. The tree sprites come from rainbowroar14's own Sprout repo, and the signpost is the user's reference image.
